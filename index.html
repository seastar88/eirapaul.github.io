About

Writer. Dreamer. Occasional victim of altitude.

Eira Paul is a Colorado-based writer who sometimes lets the thin air of the Rockies go to her head.

Her work is shaped by big skies, winding roads, and the strange stories that gather in quiet places. When she is not writing, she can usually be found chasing a view, collecting ideas, or pretending she went outside for reasons unrelated to a plot.
